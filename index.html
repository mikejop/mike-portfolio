BIOGRAPHY

Cinematographer & Colorist

Michael Oliveira is a Brazilian filmmaker, cinematographer, and colorist based in São Paulo. His work explores the intersection of cinematic atmosphere, emotional realism, and visual precision, combining cinematography and post-production into a unified storytelling language.

With over two decades of experience in filmmaking and color finishing, Oliveira developed a visual approach strongly influenced by his background in post-production, where color, contrast, and texture become essential narrative elements rather than technical corrections.

Working across digital cinema and analog film, his images are defined by controlled lighting, immersive tonal palettes, and emotionally driven composition — always in service of the story's emotional core.

PURPOSE

Passion for Success

His purpose as an audiovisual professional is to reveal, through each project, the intrinsic beauty of the stories entrusted to him, even when resources are limited.

He values careful planning, precise composition, and sensitive cinematography, adapting accessible equipment to the available budget. He commits to extracting the maximum from every possibility, turning restrictions into creative opportunities and delivering powerful, memorable visual narratives.

EXPERTISE

SKILLS & CAPABILITIES

Cinematography

Director of photography with over 20 years of experience in the audiovisual industry, specializing in creating cinematic images even in low-budget productions. Works in advertising, branded content, music videos, and fictional narratives, turning technical limitations into visual language.

Color Grading

Senior colorist and Color Grading instructor at EBAC (British School of Creative Arts and Technology), with experience in advertising campaigns, documentaries, and digital content. Specialist in aesthetic construction, visual consistency, and professional post-production workflows in DaVinci Resolve.

Directing

Creative director with experience in advertising campaigns, branded content, and original projects. Specialized in developing cinematic atmospheres, visual direction, and emotional storytelling for brands, artists, and independent productions.

Editing & Post-Production

Editor and post-production supervisor with experience in projects for Banco do Brasil, Shell, Embratur, and Sabin Laboratories. Works in structuring workflows, narrative editing, finishing, and technical delivery for cinema, TV, and digital platforms.

Mentorships & Training

Audiovisual instructor and mentor offering professional courses in filming and advanced color grading. Combines classroom leadership (Color Grading professor at EBAC - British School of Creative Arts and Technology) with personalized training programs (Mentoria em Direção de Fotografia & Color Grading) in São Paulo, helping colorists and filmmakers structure bulletproof workflows.
